01

Initial Consultation

Your journey begins in our private salon, where you'll meet with our creative director. We'll discuss your vision, the occasion, your personal style, and any specific details that inspire you.

This intimate conversation forms the foundation of your bespoke gown, ensuring every element reflects your unique elegance.

02

Design & Fabric Selection

Our master couturiers will create original sketches based on your consultation. You'll select from our exclusive collection of the world's finest fabrics—silks from Como, laces from Calais, and embellishments from Parisian ateliers.

Each material is hand-selected for its exceptional quality and ability to bring your vision to life.

03

Measurements & Toile

Our expert tailors will take precise measurements, creating a muslin toile (prototype) to perfect the fit and silhouette. This ensures your gown will drape flawlessly, celebrating your unique form.

Multiple fittings guarantee absolute perfection in every detail.

04

Artisanal Creation

In our Parisian atelier, master craftsmen dedicate over 200 hours to bringing your gown to life. Every stitch is executed by hand, every embellishment placed with precision.

This is where traditional savoir-faire meets your unique vision, creating a masterpiece that will be treasured for generations.

05

Final Fitting & Delivery

Return for your final fitting where any last adjustments are made to ensure absolute perfection. We'll guide you on how to wear and care for your gown.

Your completed masterpiece is presented in a bespoke garment bag, ready to make your most important moments unforgettable.
