Hand Embroidery

Lunéville & Traditional Techniques

Our embroidery artisans are masters of the legendary Lunéville technique, a centuries-old method that allows for the most intricate and delicate embellishments. Using a specialized crochet hook, each bead, sequin, and crystal is individually applied to create patterns of breathtaking complexity.

A single evening gown may feature over 50,000 hand-applied embellishments, requiring up to 300 hours of focused embroidery work. We source our materials from the finest suppliers: Swarovski crystals from Austria, Japanese seed beads, freshwater pearls, and semi-precious stones.

  • Lunéville embroidery technique
  • Traditional tambour work
  • Flat embroidery (point de beauvais)
  • Goldwork and threadwork
  • Stumpwork and raised embroidery

The Process

200+ Hours of Artistry

Each GHOHARY creation undergoes an extensive journey from initial sketch to final masterpiece. Our process honors traditional haute couture methods while incorporating modern precision where it enhances quality.

01

Design & Consultation

Private consultation, sketch creation, and fabric selection tailored to your vision and requirements. 8-12 hours.

02

Pattern Making

Master pattern makers create bespoke patterns based on your exact measurements and desired silhouette. 12-16 hours.

03

Toile Fitting

Initial muslin prototype fitting to perfect the shape, drape, and proportions before touching precious fabrics. 6-8 hours.

04

Cutting & Assembly

Precision cutting of final fabrics and initial garment assembly using traditional couture techniques. 24-30 hours.

05

Embroidery & Embellishment

Hand embroidery, beadwork, and crystal application—the most time-intensive phase. 80-150 hours.

06

Multiple Fittings

Two to three fittings to ensure absolute perfection in fit, movement, and comfort. 8-12 hours.

07

Finishing & Quality Control

Final hand-finishing, lining, hem work, and rigorous quality inspection. 20-25 hours.

08

Delivery

White-glove delivery in custom garment case with care instructions and certificate of authenticity.

Our Standards

Uncompromising Excellence

Perfect Seams

Every seam is hand-basted before final stitching, ensuring absolute precision. We use 14-16 stitches per inch—twice the industry standard.

French Seams

All interior seams are finished with French seam techniques, enclosing raw edges for a clean, couture finish that lasts decades.

Hand-Sewn Buttonholes

Buttonholes are created entirely by hand using buttonhole twist silk thread, taking up to 45 minutes per buttonhole.

Natural Materials

We use only natural fibers, mother-of-pearl buttons, silk linings, and horsehair canvas for structure and longevity.

Individual Attention

Each garment is assigned to a lead artisan who oversees every aspect of its creation from start to finish.

Final Inspection

Before leaving our atelier, every creation undergoes a 23-point quality inspection by our master couturier.
