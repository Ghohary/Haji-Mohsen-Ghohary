International Shipping

Customs & Import Duties

When shipping internationally, customs duties and import taxes may apply based on your country's regulations. These fees are the responsibility of the recipient.

What We Handle

  • Complete and accurate customs documentation
  • Commercial invoice with proper valuation
  • Classification codes (HS codes)
  • Certificate of origin when required
  • All export documentation from France

Your Responsibility

  • Import duties and taxes charged by your country
  • VAT or GST (where applicable)
  • Customs clearance fees
  • Brokerage fees (if applicable)

These fees are collected by the courier upon delivery or during customs clearance.

Common Questions

Shipping FAQs

When will my order ship?

Bespoke garments ship upon completion, typically 3-6 months from final fitting. You'll receive 2 weeks advance notice before shipping with tracking details.

Can I specify a delivery date?

Yes, for white-glove courier service. Standard shipping can be scheduled within a 2-3 day window based on courier availability.

What if I'm not home for delivery?

Signature is required. If you're unavailable, the courier will leave a notice and attempt redelivery or hold at local depot for pickup.

Do you ship to P.O. boxes?

No, we require a physical street address for signature-required delivery. We can ship to your workplace or hotel if preferred.

What if my package is delayed in customs?

We monitor all shipments and will assist with any customs issues. Our team has extensive experience navigating international customs procedures.

Is the garment case included?

Yes, our signature garment case is included with every purchase and serves as both shipping container and long-term storage solution.
