NOTRE HISTOIRE

A Legacy of
Uncompromising
Excellence

Since 1898, Maison Valmont has been the epitome of haute couture excellence. Founded in the heart of Paris by master couturier François Valmont, our atelier has dressed royalty, celebrities, and discerning women who demand nothing but perfection.

Each creation is the culmination of over 200 hours of meticulous handwork, employing techniques passed down through five generations of master artisans. We source the world's finest fabrics from Como, Lyon, and St. Gallen, ensuring that every thread embodies luxury.
