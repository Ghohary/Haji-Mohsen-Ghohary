Our Culture

A Sanctuary of Excellence

The GHOHARY atelier is more than a workplace—it is a community of master artisans united by a shared pursuit of beauty and perfection. We foster an environment where traditional craftsmanship is honored, skills are continually refined, and each team member's expertise is deeply valued.

Working at GHOHARY means dedicating yourself to creating garments that will be treasured for generations. It means never compromising on quality, taking pride in every stitch, and knowing that your work represents the pinnacle of sartorial art.

  • Small, dedicated team of master artisans
  • Apprenticeship and continuous learning programs
  • Natural light-filled Parisian studios
  • Five-week annual vacation
  • Health and wellness benefits
  • Annual atelier retreat in Provence

Our Standards

Application Process

How to Apply

We carefully review every application. Please allow 2-3 weeks for our team to assess your qualifications.

01

Submit Application

Complete the form below with your CV, cover letter, and portfolio (for artisan positions).

02

Initial Review

Our team reviews your qualifications and portfolio. Qualified candidates are contacted within 2-3 weeks.

03

Skills Assessment

For artisan positions, a practical skills test is required at our atelier.

04

Interview

In-person interviews with our creative director and atelier leadership.

05

Decision

Successful candidates receive an offer within one week of the final interview.
