Care Guide

Preserving Your Investment

Your GHOHARY creation is a work of art crafted to last for generations. With proper care and attention, your garment will retain its beauty and structural integrity for decades. Follow these expert guidelines to preserve your investment.

Essential Practices

General Care Principles

Handle with Clean Hands

Always ensure hands are clean and free from lotions, oils, or perfumes before handling your garment.

Apply Products First

Always apply makeup, perfume, and hairspray before dressing to avoid staining delicate fabrics.

Immediate Attention

Address any stains or spills immediately by blotting gently—never rub. Contact us for guidance.

Air After Wearing

Allow your garment to air for 24 hours before storage to release moisture and prevent odor.

Professional Care Only

Never attempt home cleaning. Our garments require specialist haute couture cleaning services.

Rotate Storage

If storing for extended periods, remove and air your garment every 6 months.

Storage

Proper Storage Practices

Correct storage is essential to maintaining the shape, structure, and beauty of your GHOHARY creation. Our garments are delivered in custom storage solutions designed specifically for haute couture.

Short-Term Storage (Between Wears)

  • Hang on provided padded hanger to maintain shoulder shape
  • Use breathable cotton garment bag (provided with purchase)
  • Store in cool, dry closet away from direct sunlight
  • Keep away from heat sources and humidity
  • Ensure adequate space—never crush between other garments

Long-Term Storage (Seasonal/Extended)

  • Have professionally cleaned before storage (GHOHARY-approved cleaners only)
  • Store flat in acid-free tissue paper in provided archival box
  • Place in climate-controlled environment (60-70°F, 40-50% humidity)
  • Never use plastic bags or covers—fabrics must breathe
  • Include cedar sachets for natural moth protection
  • Inspect and refold every 6 months to prevent permanent creases

Professional Cleaning

Cleaning Recommendations

GHOHARY garments require specialized haute couture cleaning methods. We maintain relationships with expert cleaners worldwide who understand the delicate nature of hand-embroidered, beaded, and embellished couture.

When to Clean

Evening gowns and special occasion wear should be professionally cleaned:

  • After each wearing if stained or soiled
  • After 2-3 wears if no visible stains
  • Before seasonal storage
  • Immediately if exposed to spills

Approved Cleaners

If using a local cleaner, ensure they:

  • Specialize in haute couture/wedding gowns
  • Hand-clean embellishments separately
  • Use gentle, pH-neutral solvents
  • Never use standard dry cleaning machines
  • Provide references for beaded/embroidered work

Contact us for approved cleaner recommendations in your area.

What to Avoid

Never expose your garment to:

  • Home washing machines or hand washing
  • Standard dry cleaning (too harsh)
  • Bleach or harsh chemicals
  • Direct steaming on embellishments
  • Hot irons directly on fabric
  • Spot removal products
Embellishments

Caring for Beading & Embroidery

The hand-applied embellishments on your GHOHARY garment represent hundreds of hours of meticulous work. These delicate details require special attention and care.

Handling Embellished Areas

  • Never pull, tug, or catch embellishments on jewelry or accessories
  • Avoid sitting on heavily beaded areas when possible
  • If a bead or crystal becomes loose, save it and contact us for reattachment
  • Store embellished garments with tissue paper protecting beadwork
  • Avoid contact with rough surfaces that could snag threadwork

Professional Inspection

We recommend annual inspection of embellished garments at our atelier. We will:

  • Check all embellishments for secure attachment
  • Reinforce any loose beading or embroidery
  • Clean and restore luster to crystals and beads
  • Assess overall condition and provide care recommendations

This service is complimentary for the first 5 years after purchase.

Seasonal Guidance

Seasonal Care Tips

Spring/Summer Care

  • Monitor for increased humidity in storage areas
  • Use dehumidifier if necessary (40-50% humidity ideal)
  • Protect from direct sunlight which can fade colors
  • Air lightweight silks seasonally to prevent moisture buildup
  • Be extra cautious with perspiration—have cleaned promptly

Fall/Winter Care

  • Ensure velvet and heavier fabrics have adequate air circulation
  • Avoid storing near heating vents or radiators
  • Check for moth activity and use natural cedar deterrents
  • Brush velvet gently with specialized brush to maintain pile
  • Store away from artificial Christmas trees which can snag delicate fabrics
Emergency Situations

Immediate Care for Accidents

Liquid Spills

DO: Blot immediately with clean, white, absorbent cloth. Work from outside of stain toward center. Contact us or approved cleaner within 24 hours.

DON'T: Rub, scrub, or apply water or cleaning solutions.

Food or Grease

DO: Gently lift away solid matter with clean spoon or knife edge. Blot lightly. Seek professional cleaning immediately.

DON'T: Apply water, which can spread grease stains.

Makeup or Cosmetics

DO: Bring to professional cleaner as soon as possible—specify type of cosmetic product.

DON'T: Attempt to remove—makeup contains oils that spread easily.

Tears or Damage

DO: Stop wearing immediately. Store carefully. Contact GHOHARY atelier for repair assessment.

DON'T: Attempt to sew or repair yourself—specialized techniques required.
