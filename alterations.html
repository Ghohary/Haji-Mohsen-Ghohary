Included Services

Complimentary Alterations

Within the first two years of purchase, all alterations and adjustments are complimentary, provided the garment is in good condition and changes are within the structural capabilities of the design.

Hem Adjustments

Lengthening or shortening hems to achieve the perfect length with your preferred shoes. Hand-finished to match original couture standards.

Bodice Fitting

Taking in or letting out the bodice for changes in fit. Includes bust, waist, and torso adjustments.

Sleeve Modifications

Adjusting sleeve length or width while maintaining the original drape and proportion of the design.

Strap Adjustments

Lengthening, shortening, or repositioning straps for optimal comfort and fit.

Side Seam Alterations

Precision adjustments to side seams for a closer or more relaxed fit through the body.

Minor Neckline Changes

Subtle adjustments to neckline depth or width within the design's original vision.

Possibilities

Alteration Capabilities

Our in-house tailoring team can accommodate most alterations while preserving the artistic integrity of the original design. However, some modifications may have limitations based on the garment's construction and embellishment placement.

Standard Alterations

  • Hem length adjustments (up or down)
  • Taking in or letting out seams (up to 2 sizes)
  • Sleeve length modifications
  • Strap length adjustments
  • Minor bodice adjustments
  • Waist suppression or release
  • Bust adjustments
  • Shoulder width modifications

Complex Alterations

These require more extensive work and may have associated fees after the 2-year complimentary period:

  • Significant size changes (more than 2 sizes)
  • Neckline redesign
  • Adding or removing sleeves
  • Restructuring the bodice
  • Major embellishment repositioning
  • Silhouette alterations

Common Questions

Alteration FAQs

How long do alterations take?

Standard alterations require 7-10 business days. Rush services are available for urgent needs.

Are alterations really complimentary?

Yes, all standard alterations within the first 2 years are complimentary. After 2 years, reasonable fees apply based on the complexity of work.

Can you make my dress bigger?

Yes, within limits. Our garments are constructed with generous seam allowances allowing for size increases up to approximately 2 sizes.

What if I've gained or lost significant weight?

We can accommodate significant size changes, though extensive restructuring may have associated fees. Schedule a consultation to assess possibilities.

Can alterations damage my dress?

No. Our artisans use the same couture techniques employed in the original construction, ensuring alterations maintain the garment's integrity and beauty.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes, all fittings are by appointment only to ensure dedicated time with our master tailors.
