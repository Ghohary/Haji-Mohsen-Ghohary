1. What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your computer or mobile device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make websites work more efficiently and provide information to website owners.

Cookies serve various purposes, such as:

  • Remembering your preferences and settings
  • Enabling essential website functionality
  • Analyzing how visitors use the website
  • Delivering personalized content and advertisements
  • Improving website performance and user experience

2. Why We Use Cookies

GHOHARY uses cookies to:

  • Ensure Website Functionality: Enable core features such as security, network management, and accessibility
  • Enhance User Experience: Remember your preferences, such as language and currency selection
  • Analyze Performance: Understand how visitors interact with our website to improve our services
  • Personalize Content: Deliver relevant content and recommendations based on your interests
  • Marketing and Advertising: Show you relevant advertisements and measure the effectiveness of our campaigns
  • Security: Protect against fraudulent activity and enhance website security

3. Types of Cookies We Use

We use different types of cookies, which can be categorized as follows:

3.1 By Duration

  • Session Cookies: Temporary cookies that are deleted when you close your browser. They help us remember your actions during a single browsing session.
  • Persistent Cookies: Cookies that remain on your device for a set period or until you delete them. They help us remember your preferences for future visits.

3.2 By Purpose

  • Essential Cookies: Necessary for the website to function properly
  • Analytics/Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors use our website
  • Marketing/Advertising Cookies: Used to deliver relevant advertisements
  • Preference Cookies: Remember your settings and choices

3.3 By Origin

  • First-Party Cookies: Set by GHOHARY directly
  • Third-Party Cookies: Set by our partners and service providers

9. Managing Your Cookie Preferences

You have several options to manage or disable cookies:

9.1 Cookie Consent Manager

When you first visit our website, you will see a cookie consent banner. You can accept all cookies or customize your preferences by category. You can change your preferences at any time by clicking the "Cookie Settings" link in our website footer.

9.2 Browser Settings

Most web browsers allow you to control cookies through their settings. You can set your browser to:

  • Block all cookies
  • Only accept first-party cookies
  • Delete cookies when you close your browser
  • Notify you when a website tries to set a cookie

Here's how to manage cookies in popular browsers:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Cookies and other site data
  • Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Cookies and website data
  • Firefox: Options > Privacy & Security > Cookies and Site Data
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Cookies and data stored

9.3 Opt-Out Tools

You can opt out of specific tracking and advertising cookies using these tools:

  • Google Analytics: Google Analytics Opt-out Browser Add-on
  • Network Advertising Initiative: NAI Opt-Out Tool
  • Digital Advertising Alliance: DAA Opt-Out Tool

Important Considerations

Please note that disabling cookies may affect the functionality of our website and your user experience. Some features may not work properly without cookies enabled.

10. Cookie Consent

  • Manage Preferences: Customize which types of cookies you want to accept

10.2 Changing Your Consent

You can change your cookie preferences at any time by:

10.3 Consent Duration

Your cookie consent preferences are stored for 12 months. After this period, we will ask for your consent again when you visit our website.
