Our Heritage

Five generations of master couturiers, united by an unwavering commitment to excellence and artisanal perfection.

"Excellence is not an act, but a habit passed down through generations."
— François Valmont, Founder
1898

The Beginning

François Valmont opens a small atelier in Paris, creating bespoke gowns for Parisian society. His dedication to perfection and innovative designs quickly establish the maison's reputation for excellence.

1925

International Recognition

The maison receives its first commission from European royalty. This marks the beginning of Maison Valmont's legacy as couturier to queens, princesses, and the world's most discerning women.

1950

Post-War Elegance

Under the direction of Henri Valmont, the maison embraces the new era of glamour while maintaining its commitment to traditional craftsmanship. Hollywood stars begin to discover Maison Valmont.

1978

Global Expansion

Maison Valmont opens boutiques in New York and London, bringing Parisian haute couture to the world. Each location maintains the intimate, personalized service that defines the maison.

2005

Modern Renaissance

Isabelle Valmont becomes Creative Director, honoring the maison's heritage while introducing contemporary elements. The brand gains renewed international acclaim.

2024

Continuing the Legacy

Today, Maison Valmont remains family-owned, creating exceptional gowns in the same Parisian atelier where François Valmont began over 125 years ago. Each creation upholds the founding principles of artisanal excellence and timeless elegance.

OUR PHILOSOPHY

Timeless Craftsmanship

At Maison Valmont, we believe true luxury lies in the marriage of exceptional materials, masterful technique, and unwavering dedication to perfection.

Every gown that leaves our atelier represents over 200 hours of meticulous handwork by artisans who have dedicated their lives to the art of haute couture. We source the finest fabrics from renowned textile houses in Como, Lyon, and St. Gallen, ensuring that every thread embodies luxury.
